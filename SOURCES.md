# Source register

All sources below were accessed on **2026-09-20**. They are official artist, government, language-institute, vendor, or original research/publisher sources. Statements in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) distinguish documented features from proposed methods and untested inferences. Public pages can change; recheck rights, pricing, model-specific terms, and features before production. No audio examples were downloaded or used.

## S01

[Johnny Cash official album discography](https://www.johnnycash.com/music/american-iv-the-man-comes-around/) and [official Hurt track credits](https://www.johnnycash.com/track/hurt-2/). Accessed 2026-09-20. Establish the album's November 5, 2002 release, the separate single/video context, and Trent Reznor's songwriting credit. The track page contains lyrics; none are reproduced here. Credits do not establish present licensing authority or an exact digital master.

## S02

[U.S. Copyright Office, Circular 56A: Musical Compositions and Sound Recordings](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ56a.pdf). Accessed 2026-09-20. Explains the distinction between a musical work and a particular sound recording. Not a clearance decision for this project.

## S03

[U.S. Copyright Office, Circular 14: Derivative Works and Compilations](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ14.pdf). Accessed 2026-09-20. Identifies translations and musical arrangements as derivative-work examples and discusses underlying rights. Does not establish permission for this adaptation.

## S04

[U.S. Copyright Office, Title 17, Chapter 1](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html). Accessed 2026-09-20. Relevant provisions: sections 101 and 106 for derivative works/exclusive rights; 114 for sound-recording scope; 115(a)(2) for the limited compulsory musical-arrangement privilege. Current contractual and jurisdictional analysis remains necessary.

## S05

[U.S. Copyright Office, Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 1: Digital Replicas](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-1-Digital-Replicas-Report.pdf). Published July 2024; accessed 2026-09-20. Explains recording copyright versus voice identity, publicity protections, and legal gaps. Historical report and recommendations, not a statement that no law has changed since 2024.

## S06

[Klingon Language Institute, Sounds of Klingon](https://www.kli.org/about-klingon/sounds-of-klingon/). Accessed 2026-09-20. Primary institutional pronunciation guide: meaningful case, apostrophe, multicharacter letters, and articulatory descriptions. Read as a language reference; its existence does not validate any synthesis model.

## S07

[Klingon Language Institute, Writing Klingon](https://www.kli.org/about-klingon/writing-klingon/). Accessed 2026-09-20. Distinguishes Okrand's standard orthography from other character systems. Supports using romanized working text; no font or imagery is copied.

## S08

[Klingon Language Institute, Klingon Language Certification Program](https://www.kli.org/activities/klcp/). Accessed 2026-09-20. Describes certification levels and written examinations. Credentials can help assess language knowledge but do not establish singing or oral-review proficiency.

## S09

[Peter Low, Translating the Texts of Songs and Other Vocal Music, Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-handbook-of-translation/translating-the-texts-of-songs-and-other-vocal-music/44F8DE64FDAD57464832D20272650287). Published online March 10, 2022; accessed 2026-09-20. Public chapter summary distinguishes use-specific translations and the syllable/rhythm constraints of adapting to existing melody. Only the accessible summary was used, not an assumed reading of the paywalled chapter.

## S10

[Kits AI, AI Voice Changers](https://www.kits.ai/ai-voice-changers). Accessed 2026-09-20. Documents audio upload/recording, voice conversion, and a singing voice library. Product claims are not independent quality measurements; no Klingon benchmark or Cash authorization is established.

## S11

[Kits AI, Terms of Service](https://www.kits.ai/terms-of-service). Accessed 2026-09-20 through the current pricing-page legal link. Relevant sections: 5.3(a), 6.1, 6.4, and 6.5 on unauthorized impersonation, custom-model ownership, input licenses, and prospective training opt-out. Review all applicable voice-specific terms and obtain written compatibility with the source grants before uploading. The opt-out does not retroactively undo prior training.

## S12

[Kits AI, Pricing](https://www.kits.ai/pricing). Accessed 2026-09-20 and checked in the live browser's monthly view. Displayed Producer $30/month with 60 download minutes; Professional $60/month with unlimited download minutes subject to fair use. Page states pricing is for new users. Currency, tax, checkout terms, and eligibility require confirmation. Free/Starter cards and FAQ differ on some features; the plan does not resolve that discrepancy by guessing.

## S13

[Voice-Swap, official product page](https://www.voice-swap.ai/). Accessed 2026-09-20. Separately describes Singing Studio, speech/voiceover, and custom voice models. Does not establish Klingon singing accuracy, Cash participation, or project-specific commercial permission.

## S14

[Voice-Swap, Terms and Conditions](https://www.voice-swap.ai/terms-conditions) and [Prohibited Use Policy](https://www.voice-swap.ai/prohibited-use-policy). Accessed 2026-09-20; terms also inspected in the live browser.
