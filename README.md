# hurt

Planning a Klingon-language rendition of **Hurt** in the style of Johnny Cash's cover. **Planning only; no audio produced.**

**Current direction, v2:** an original fictional Klingon vocalist sings a recognizable version of the song, with newly performed or generated instrumentation and a musical structure/mood inspired by Cash's cover. Cash's voice and the original recording are no longer production targets. The project is noncommercial and intended as parody; the actual commentary remains an artistic decision, and fair use is not assumed.

- [Current plan: v2](PLAN-v2.md): affordable DIY workflow, human and synthetic vocal options, future pilot, recognition/language checks, and fair-use versus permission routes.
- [Historical plan: v1](PLAN.md): retained for reference; its Cash-voice, original-backing, and professional-budget assumptions are superseded.
- [Source register](SOURCES.md): primary and official research, accessed September 20, 2026, with evidence limits.

The recommended route uses the owner's or another consenting person's singing voice, light character processing if useful, and newly recorded or MIDI-rendered backing. Start with owned equipment and a small pilot **only after separate production approval**. No Cash or actor voice imitation, original-master sampling, or stem extraction is needed.

This public repository contains planning documents only. It contains no lyrics or translations, recordings or stems, voice models, production software, or private permission records. Publication of this plan does not authorize production, purchases, outreach, or release, and does not establish legal clearance.
